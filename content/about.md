I am an incoming PhD Student in [Biostatistics](https://www.public-health.uiowa.edu/biostat/) at the University of Iowa College of Public Health.

In 2026, I graduated from Grinnell College with a [major in mathematics](https://www.grinnell.edu/academics/majors-concentrations/math) (honors) and a statistics concentration. As a student, I worked as a tutor in the [Math Lab](https://www.grinnell.edu/academics/majors-concentrations/math/math-lab), a mentor in the college's [Data Analysis and Social Inquiry Lab](https://www.grinnell.edu/academics/centers-programs/data-analysis-and-social-inquiry-lab), and a grader for the statistics department. I also served on the Statistics SEPC, a student government body that assists in the faculty hiring and review process within the department, as well as the planning of extracurricular activities.
